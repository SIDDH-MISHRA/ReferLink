Create my referral link

Free · No sign-up · Under a minute

How it works

  1. Add your link to LinkedIn.
    "Want a referral from me? Request it here."
  2. Candidates fill a short form.
    Job link, resume and why they fit. Incomplete requests can't be sent.
  3. You tap Referred or Decline.
    They see the answer on their own status page. No more ghosting.

Safe by design

No passwords. You get a private link instead. A candidate's details are shown only to the employee they asked, and they can delete them at any time.

How we handle data

Looking for a referral?

Each employee has their own ReferLink. Look for it on their LinkedIn profile, or ask them for it.
