ReferLink

Privacy, in plain words

ReferLink is a small side project. Here's exactly what happens to your data.

What we collect

  • Employees: name, company, LinkedIn URL, and an optional list of roles and a note.
  • Candidates: name, email, LinkedIn URL, job link or ID, resume link, and "why you fit".

We don't store resume files. Your resume stays wherever you keep it, and you can turn off sharing at any time.

Who sees it

  • A candidate's details are shown only to the employee they sent the request to.
  • An employee's name, company, note and LinkedIn link are shown on their public request page.
  • There are no passwords. Instead, there are private links. Anyone with a private link can open it, so keep yours safe.

Analytics

We count events like "request submitted" to see if ReferLink works. We use PostHog for this. These events don't include names, emails or links, and private links are removed before anything is sent. PostHog keeps an anonymous ID in your browser's storage.

Deleting your data

  • Candidates: open your status page and tap Delete my request. Your name, email, links and message are removed for good. We keep only an anonymous record (dates and outcome) so our counts stay accurate.
  • Employees: to remove a board, contact the maker of ReferLink, Siddh Mishra.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your data with anyone else.
  • Send you marketing emails.
  • Use AI to screen or rank you.
